CapEx vs OpEx

What is the difference and why does Azure use OpEx?
Answer & Explanation AZ-900 Core Concept

CapEx is upfront physical expenditure on data centers. OpEx is a consumption-based pricing model where you pay only for what you use, eliminating upfront infrastructure costs.
